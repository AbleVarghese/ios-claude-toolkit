# Pricing Strategy -- iOS Claude Toolkit (Internal)

**Not for customers. Internal analysis only.**

---

## Recommended Price: $39

### Launch Price: $29 (first 50 customers)

After 50 sales or 2 weeks (whichever comes first), raise to $39.

---

## Reasoning

### 1. Developer Tool Sweet Spot

$39 sits in impulse-buy territory for professional developers. It's the cost of a decent lunch for two. No one needs to open a purchase order, get manager approval, or justify it on an expense report. Compare: a senior iOS developer earns $150-200/hr. If the toolkit saves 30 minutes once, it's paid for itself.

### 2. Value Calculation

- 21 skills, each averaging ~2 hours to research, write, test, and refine from scratch
- Total development time: ~42 hours
- At $150/hr consulting rate: $6,300 of development effort
- $39 = 0.6% of the value delivered
- Even at a conservative 10x markdown from development cost, $39 is well under $630

### 3. Market Comparables

| Product Type | Price Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| VS Code extension packs | $0-15 | Commodity, low switching cost |
| Notion/Figma templates | $15-39 | Closest comparable in format |
| Developer starter kits | $29-79 | Rails starters, Next.js boilerplates |
| SaaS boilerplates | $199-499 | Full codebases, higher complexity |
| Cursor rule packs | $19-49 | Direct comparable (AI IDE config) |
| iOS project templates | $49-99 | Xcode templates with architecture |

The iOS Claude Toolkit is more specialized than generic templates but less complex than full boilerplates. $39 positions it at the upper end of templates and lower end of starter kits -- appropriate for its depth and specificity.

### 4. Psychological Pricing

- $29 launch price creates urgency ("limited early price") and lowers the barrier for first adopters who haven't seen reviews yet
- $39 regular price stays below the $40 threshold where buyers start to hesitate
- "Pay what you want" with $29 minimum captures developers who want to pay more (common on Gumroad -- average overpay is 15-20% for developer tools)

### 5. Team License: $99

- Up to 10 developers on the same team
- 2.5x individual price for 10x the seats
- Attractive to team leads who discover it individually and want to roll it out
- No enforcement mechanism needed -- honor system (standard for Gumroad digital products)

## Risks

**Priced too low**: Developer tools often suffer from "too cheap to be good" perception. $29-39 avoids this -- it signals quality without signaling enterprise complexity. Monitor for comments like "I expected more at this price" (means price is fine) vs. "seemed too cheap to trust" (means raise it).

**Priced too high**: If refund rate exceeds 10% or conversion rate on the product page drops below 2%, consider a temporary price reduction or adding a free sample skill to reduce purchase anxiety.

**Free alternatives**: Claude Code skills are just markdown files. Anyone can write their own. The value proposition is curation, battle-testing, and time savings -- not scarcity. Price accordingly (time-saver, not gatekeeper).
